# DermCase — Conversation Log

Phase 1 premise validation. Goal: 5 dermatologist conversations on **non-trivial** real cases using the literature brief prototype.

## Success criterion (sharpened)

**Yes signal = the brief changed a management decision OR meaningfully reduced search time on a case the derm rated as non-trivial.**

Generic dermatology cases ("would have searched UpToDate in 2 minutes") do not test the hypothesis. Push for cases the derm describes as: uncommon presentation, treatment-resistant, off-label question, pregnancy/immunosuppression edge case, or any case where morphology alone is insufficient.
